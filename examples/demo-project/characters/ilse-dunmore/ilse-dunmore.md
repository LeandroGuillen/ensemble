Ilse commands the harbor levy for [[House Dunmore]]. She closes the docks to keep the drowned out, trapping living sailors as well. [[Teodor Dunmore]] calls it prudence; she privately calls it fear.

## The price of command
After the first light returns, she must ask [[Mara Vell]] for help.
