Mara tends the light at [[Vellhaven Lighthouse]], where each wick holds a borrowed memory. She promised [[Oren Vell]] she would never burn their mother’s last song. When the harbor goes dark, keeping that promise may cost a fleet.

## A keeper’s bargain
She trusts [[saffi-reed]] with the keys, but keeps the memory ledger locked.
